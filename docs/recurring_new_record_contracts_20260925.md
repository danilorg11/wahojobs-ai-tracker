# Recurring new-record qualification — 2026-09-25

This release extends only two already approved public surfaces. It does not
change the 14-source schedule or any HTTP, execution, recovery, or maintenance
ceiling. The retained Outlier and DataForce captures are the real evidence for
the validated role families; added-language tests are synthetic contract
fixtures, not newly observed employer opportunities.

## Outlier

The public board still costs one request and exposes exact numeric IDs and
public role URLs. Up to eight exact board-linked details are requested within
the nine-request daily cap. When the board includes an ID outside the original
eight, one detail slot rotates across such IDs and seven slots recheck the
original eight. An unknown ID qualifies under
`outlier_index_detail_role_family_v2` only if its board and detail agree on
ID, title, internal job ID, content, countries and location; the detail has
a role-bound signup flow; and its title and content match one of the four
families demonstrated in the retained eight records. Other identities remain
unqualified. Evergreen applications remain evergreen; role opportunities
remain public inventory, with no claim of active project work.

## DataForce

The approved project index still discovers current cards. Within the existing
15-request cap, daily details recheck the eight historical Thyme roles and
rotate remote cards from the index. A new card qualifies under
`dataforce_thyme_index_detail_family_v2` only for the evidenced remote Thyme
Freelance Writer family, with exact card identity, country, title, detail
canonical URL and heading, AI writing and remote-work text, and one role
application action on the approved TransPerfect host. Other remote cards are
inspection-only. Empty and partial source outcomes retain their existing
rules; missing cards never authorize closure from these partial observations.

The pending-ID journal reports identities without a qualifying detail **in
that run**. It does not by itself distinguish a never-qualified identity from
a previously qualified identity awaiting refresh. The source capture and
promotion records retain the definitive per-record history.

Fixed request caps also limit freshness as the board grows. Outlier reserves
one daily detail slot for IDs outside the original eight; four or more such
IDs can exceed a 72-hour verification interval. DataForce can face the same
capacity pressure if its remote-card backlog grows. Normal expiry must remain
in force; an operator must review measured capacity before claiming every
growing new-ID cohort stays continuously current. No request budget is raised
by this release.
